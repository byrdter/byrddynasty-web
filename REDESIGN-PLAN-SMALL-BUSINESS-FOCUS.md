## 🎯 TARGET PERSONAS

### Persona 1: "The Aspiring Solo Founder"
- **Demographics:** 28-45, currently employed, technical or business background
- **Goal:** Launch a profitable side business, eventually replace day job income
- **Pain Points:** Limited time, no team, can't afford employees
- **Needs:** Business ideas, implementation guides, tool recommendations
- **Content:** Business ideas, weekend launch guides, tool stack

### Persona 2: "The Bootstrapped Entrepreneur"
- **Demographics:** 25-40, already running small business (1-5 employees)
- **Goal:** Reduce costs, increase margins, scale without hiring
- **Pain Points:** Employee costs, operational overhead, time management
- **Needs:** Automation workflows, cost calculators, AI vs human comparisons
- **Content:** Workflows, ROI calculators, case studies

### Persona 3: "The Serial Builder"
- **Demographics:** 30-50, launched multiple projects, some successful
- **Goal:** Find next profitable opportunity, optimize operations
- **Pain Points:** Opportunity validation, competition, building efficiently
- **Needs:** Industry analyses, boring business ideas, efficiency frameworks
- **Content:** Industry reports, contrarian ideas, founder stories

### Persona 4: "The Technical Founder"
- **Demographics:** 25-40, developer/designer background, wants to go solo
- **Goal:** Use technical skills to build profitable business
- **Pain Points:** Business model, pricing, non-technical operations
- **Needs:** Technical tool guides, pricing strategies, business fundamentals
- **Content:** Tool deep-dives, workflow tutorials, SaaS business models

### Persona 5: "The Corporate Escapee"
- **Demographics:** 35-55, burned out on corporate life, wants freedom
- **Goal:** Build lifestyle business, work remotely, control schedule
- **Pain Points:** Risk aversion, no entrepreneurial experience, overwhelm
- **Needs:** Low-risk opportunities, step-by-step guides, realistic expectations
- **Content:** Service business models, consulting frameworks, realistic timelines
